Using a computer for long periods can affect your health if you don’t take the right precautions. Fatigue, muscle tension and mental stress are common problems, so it’s important to develop good habits that protect your body and mind. A few simple changes can make your daily work much more comfortable and healthier.

First, you should adjust your screen to a comfortable height so your neck stays straight. Make sure to sit with your back supported and keep your feet flat on the floor. To protect your eyes, it’s important to take short breaks every 20 minutes and look at a distant point. You must avoid working in dim lighting, as this increases eye strain. Don’t forget to stretch your shoulders, arms and legs regularly to reduce stiffness. Finally, you should schedule brief pauses during the day to relax, breathe deeply and reduce stress.

Following these tips will help you stay healthy while working on a computer. Your health is important, so take care of yourself!
